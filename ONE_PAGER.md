## The Problem

Egypt's real estate market is **fragmented, opaque, and untrustworthy**.

- **12M+ housing units needed by 2030** — but buyers search across Facebook groups, OLX, WhatsApp, and word-of-mouth
- **Zero verification** — fake photos, phantom listings, hidden "service fees" at signing
- **Cairo-centric platforms** — Red Sea, Upper Egypt, New Capital buyers are ignored
- **No comparison tools** — can't save, compare, or share shortlists

> *"I lost 50k EGP deposit on a listing that didn't exist. Never again."* — Cairo buyer, 2024

---

## The Solution: manyhouses

**One verified marketplace for all of Egypt.**

| Feature | What It Does |
|---------|--------------|
| 🛡️ **Verified Listings** | Every property photographed, ownership confirmed, pricing transparent |
| 🗺️ **National Coverage** | 10+ cities: Cairo, Giza, Alexandria, Hurghada, Sharm El Sheikh, Luxor, Aswan, Marsa Alam, Siwa, New Administrative Capital |
| ❤️ **Smart Favorites** | Save properties you like with one click — no login required |
| 📄 **Instant Contracts** | Generate a contract draft in-app, downloaded as a text file |
| 🔍 **Unified Search** | Filter by city, type, price, rooms, area — instant results |
| 📍 **Map Integration** | See every property on map with neighborhood context |
