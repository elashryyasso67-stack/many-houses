# manyhouses — Demo Video Script

**Length:** about 90 seconds. Record the real app in a browser.

| Time | Show | Say |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | The sign-in page | "This is manyhouses, a place to find property across Egypt." |
| 0:10 | Type any email and password, then Sign in | "Sign in takes you straight to the listings." |
| 0:20 | Search "villa" | "Search by name, city or type." |
| 0:35 | Open a property | "Each property has its price, details and a map." |
| 0:50 | Click Add to Favorites, then open Favorites | "Save what you like. Your list lasts for this visit." |
| 1:05 | Open Contract, fill in the form, download the draft | "Make a contract draft and download it as a text file. A lawyer should review it before you sign." |
| 1:25 | Show the home page | "Try it at __________." |

Before you record: check the form fields are filled in, and that the photos have loaded.
