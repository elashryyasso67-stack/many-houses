## Recording Tips

### Screen Recording Settings
- **OBS:** Canvas 1920×1080, Source: Window Capture (Browser), 30fps, CBR 8000 Kbps
- **Loom:** "Full Screen" mode, HD recording
- **QuickTime:** New Screen Recording → Options → 1080p, High Quality

### Voiceover Options
**Option A: Record Live (Fastest)**
- Narrate while clicking
- Pros: Natural timing, one take
- Cons: Harder to edit, "umms" happen

**Option B: Record Screen First, Voiceover After (Recommended)**
- Record silent screen capture following shot list exactly
- Import to editor (CapCut, DaVinci, iMovie, Premiere)
- Record voiceover watching playback
- Pros: Perfect sync, clean audio, easy retakes
- Cons: Extra step

**Option C: Hybrid**
- Record screen + scratch audio live
- Re-record voiceover for final version
- Best of both

### Mouse & UI Polish
- **Mouse size:** Increase in OS settings (easier to follow)
- **Click highlights:** Enable in OBS/Loom
- **Smooth scroll:** Use trackpad or smooth-scroll extension
- **Hide bookmarks bar:** `Ctrl+Shift+B` (Chrome)
- **Zoom:** 100% (not 125%+)

### Editing Notes
- **Speed up typing:** 2-3x in editor for form fills
- **Cut dead air:** Remove pauses >1s
- **Add captions:** Burn in (accessibility + silent viewing)
- **Background music:** Subtle, instrumental, low volume (-20dB)
- **Color grade:** Slight warmth to match gold brand
- **Intro/outro:** 2s logo animation (Canva → export → prepend/append)
