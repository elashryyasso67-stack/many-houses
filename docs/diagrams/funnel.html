Honest gap

The app can't count yet

The steps from Explore to Act happen inside the app, which has no counter today. Social insights and QR scans can be counted from day one. An in-app counter is on the roadmap.

First-month targets

Reach 5,000 · Visit 300 · Explore 150 · Save 60 · Act 20 · founding partners 5. Only Reach, Visit and partners can be counted today.

Each step keeps only some people from the step above. That is why the bars get narrower. The biggest drop shows where to improve first.
