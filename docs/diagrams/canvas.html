Business Model Canvasنموذج العمل التجاري

near-zero budget · nationwide

Key partnersالشركاء

  • Local brokers and owners in each city (the first real listings)
  • Streamlit Community Cloud (free hosting for the live app)
  • A lawyer to review the contract template (next)
  • Student and community groups for events

Key activitiesالأنشطة

  • Sign up partner brokers, city by city
  • Add and check real listings
  • Post content every week
  • Build the roadmap features

Key resourcesالموارد

  • A working app: 10 screens, 10 cities
  • The gold house-and-key brand
  • The student team's time

Value propositionsالقيمة المقدمة

  • Searchers: every city in one search, with price, rooms and area on every card
  • A shortlist of favorites, and a map pin for each home
  • A contract draft to read before meeting anyone
  • Brokers: a place where small cities are not buried under Cairo [37]

Relationshipsالعلاقة مع العملاء

  • Self-service web app, no sign-up needed to browse
  • Personal visits to founding partners
  • WhatsApp contact (next)

Channelsالقنوات

  • Facebook groups and pages
  • Instagram posts and stories
  • QR codes at events and brokers' offices

Customer segmentsشرائح العملاء

  • Buyers: newly married couples looking for a first home outside Cairo
  • Partners: local brokers and owners who bring the first real listings
  • Later: Egyptians abroad buying back home

Cost structureالتكاليف

  • Hosting: EGP 0 today (Streamlit Community Cloud)
  • Promotion: organic posts, so time instead of money
  • Later: a domain name, real photos, and a little paid reach

Revenue streamsمصادر الدخل

  • Searching is always free
  • First: founding-partner brokers list free for 6 months, then a small monthly plan
  • Then: paid featured listings at the top of a city
