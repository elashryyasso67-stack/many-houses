Strengthsنقاط القوة

  • 10 cities from day one, from Alexandria to Aswan and Siwa

  • Costs nothing to run: one free library and free hosting

Weaknessesنقاط الضعف

  • 12 sample listings, and the photos are random placeholders
  • Prices show in $, not EGP. Nothing is checked or verified yet
  • No real accounts. Favorites end when the tab closes
  • Contact us sends nothing, and the app can't count visits
