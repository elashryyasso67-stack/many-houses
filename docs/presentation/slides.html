01 · The problem المشكلة

Cairo gets the portals. Everyone else gets Facebook.

01

Scattered

Homes are spread across brokers, Facebook groups and five or more portals. Buyers check them one by one.

02

Cairo-first

About 157k of Dubizzle's 200k+ property ads are in Cairo [37]. Property Finder's featured projects start at EGP 12.4M [32].

03

Hard to trust

Prices hidden "in private", duplicate and fake listings. The government launched a platform in 2025 to remove them [24].
