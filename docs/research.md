Research for the manyhouses pitch (Afro-Asian Forum, Marketing category). Compiled 16 October 2026.

How to read this file:

- Every fact has a number like [3]. That number points to the **Sources** list at the end.
- **(check)** means one of two things. Either only one source confirms the figure, or the data is older than 2022. Say "about" or "one estimate says" when you quote it.
- Company numbers (visits, users, listings) are what the companies **say about themselves**. Nobody audits them.

## 1. Market | السوق

### How big is it?

- **Market value.** Mordor Intelligence puts Egypt's residential real estate market at **US$9.81 billion in 2025**. It expects **US$10.71 billion in 2026** and **US$16.55 billion by 2031**, growing about **9.09% a year** [9]. Ahram Online repeats these figures, but they are still one firm's estimate [10]. Other estimates are much bigger (for example, US$20–22 billion for 2025 in Invest-Gate articles), so market-size numbers depend on how each firm measures (check).
- **Developer sales.** The top 20 developers sold about **EGP 1.6 trillion** worth of homes in 2025. That is **+10%** in value, but the **number of units fell 7%** to about 68,000 [11], [12]. Talaat Moustafa Group (TMG) was first [12].
- **The market is cooling.** In Q1 2026, top-10 developer sales fell **6.5%** to about EGP 271 billion, and units fell about 15% [13]. Good point for the pitch: when sales are harder, developers and brokers need cheaper ways to find buyers.
- **Where the value is.** Greater Cairo holds about **43.9%** of market value. About **62%** of sales are new primary units from developers [9] (check).

### Who needs a home? (demand)

- **Population:** **108 million** inside Egypt (16 Aug 2025). It took 287 days to add the last million, slower than before because there are fewer births [1]. Egyptian Streets reported **109 million** on 9 May 2026 [2] (check).
  - Note: DataReportal uses a UN estimate of **119 million** [5]. Use the CAPMAS number in the pitch. It is the official count of people inside Egypt.
- **Young country:** median age **24.5** [5]. Mordor says about **30%** of residents are aged 18–35 [9] (check).
- **New households:** there were **936,739** marriage contracts in 2024, down 2.5% from 961,220 in 2023 [3], [4]. 57.8% were in rural areas and 42.2% in cities [3]. Each marriage usually needs a flat, which is a big cultural driver of demand.
- **Homes needed per year:** estimates vary a lot, so give a range. The Housing Minister committed to **400,000 units a year** [47]. Older statements by Mostafa Madbouly (then Housing Minister) said **500,000 a year** [46] (check). Other estimates run from 300,000 to 750,000 (check).

### Cities and new cities

- **Urbanisation:** only about **43%** of Egyptians live in urban areas (43.5% per DataReportal [5], 43.1% for 2023 per UN-Habitat/World Bank [8]). So more than half the country lives outside big cities.
- **New Administrative Capital (NAC):** about **48,000 civil servants** moved there in 2024, but only about **1,200 families** lived in units by mid-2024 [9] (check). The government has also planned **20 "fourth-generation" cities** for about 30 million people since 2018 [53] (check, 2018 plan).
- **State housing:** "Housing for All Egyptians" had completed **809,185 units** by May 2026 [9] (check).
- **Why it matters for manyhouses:** listings on big portals are packed into Greater Cairo. On Dubizzle, Cairo has about **156,690** property ads and New Cairo alone has about **89,580**, out of "200,000+" [37]. Smaller cities like Aswan barely appear. manyhouses already covers 10 cities.

### Property as a "safe box" for money (inflation and the pound)

- **Devaluation:** on 6 March 2024 the Central Bank let the pound float. It fell from about **EGP 31** to about **EGP 49.5–50.6 per dollar**, alongside an expanded IMF programme of about US$8 billion [16], [17].
- **Inflation today:** urban inflation was **13.9%** in September 2026, and core inflation was **14.1%** [14], [15]. The CBE aims for **7% ± 2** in the second half of 2027 [14].
- **Store of value:** analysts call property a hedge against inflation and a weaker pound [18] (check, 2022). In Q3 2023, JLL said fear of devaluation pushed prices up about **40% in Sixth of October** and **45% in New Cairo** [19].
- **Money from abroad:** remittances hit a record **US$36.5 billion** in FY2024/25 [20], [21], and about **US$37.5 billion** in calendar 2025 [54]. Foreign purchases of Egyptian real estate rose **186%** to about **US$868 million** in Q1 2025 (CBE data via Invest-Gate) [22] (check).
- **Mortgages are expensive:** bank mortgages cost about **24.5%** a year, even after rate cuts. Subsidised 3% mortgages exist only for cheaper homes [9] (check). That is why developer installment plans matter (see Section 3).

### Phones and internet

- **98.2 million** internet users (**82.7%**) [5]. **121 million** mobile connections [5].
- **96.26 million** active mobile broadband subscriptions in May 2026 (Ministry of ICT) [7].
- **51.6 million** social media users [5].
- No official count of **smartphones** exists. GSMA data says **95.4%** of mobile connections are "broadband-capable" [6] (check).

## 2. Competitors | المنافسون

### Aqarmap (عقارماب)

- **What it is:** an Egyptian property portal. It is one of the oldest in Egypt.
- **Key features:** price guide (price per m² by area), compounds and new launches, compound and developer ratings, "Ask Neighbors", market research, real estate demand index, AI property valuation, Android and iOS apps [28].
- **How it earns:** subscriptions from real estate professionals for listing properties, and advertising packages for more visibility [29]. In 2016, individual ads cost about **EGP 100–400** [30] (check, 2016). Its expos charge developers a success fee when contracts are signed (check, 2021). Current prices are **not public**. The site only shows "Aqarmap Packages" [28].
- **Scale:** its homepage says **247,126 listings** and **1M+ active users** [28]. Statista describes it as having over 400,000 listings and about 2 million monthly visits [31] (check). It raised a Series A from Wamda Capital and others (amount not disclosed) [29] (check, ~2018).
- **Gap to target:** strong in Cairo compounds and data tools. Its pricing for small brokers is not transparent, and it is busy and heavy for a first-time user.

### Property Finder Egypt (بروبرتي فايندر)

- **What it is:** the Egypt arm of a Dubai-based portal that runs in about 8 countries [33].
- **Key features:** buy, rent and new-project search with filters, app, new-project cards with payment plans and a WhatsApp button, "Find agent", Agent Hub, "PF Expert" [32].
- **How it earns:** most revenue comes from **subscriptions sold to agents and developers** (B2B) [35]. It also offers credit-based plans, featured listings and agent tools (check: from secondary profiles). Egypt prices are **not public**.
- **Scale:** the group is very well funded. Permira and Blackstone bought a **US$525M** minority stake (Sep 2025) [34], and Ares provided **US$250M** of debt. Its UAE revenue was **US$117M in 2024** [36]. Egypt-only numbers are **unknown**. It competes with Aqarmap for traffic leadership [33].
- **Gap to target:** focused on agents and expensive new compounds. Homepage launch prices range from **EGP 12.4M to 73.5M** [32]. Ordinary flats in smaller cities are not its focus.

### Nawy (ناوي)

- **What it is:** a Cairo proptech founded in 2019. It is a **brokerage plus a listing site plus financing** [25].
- **Key features:** search by compound and developer, resale units, "Nawy Now" (move now, pay later financing), "Nawy Shares" (fractional ownership from about $500), Nawy Partners for brokers, Nawy Unlocked (property management) [25], [27]. The site lists **15,000+ properties** and **800+ compounds** [27].
- **How it earns:** it is a brokerage, so it earns from **sales and financing**. The exact commission is **not public** [25].
- **Scale:** **US$52M Series A** led by Partech, plus about **US$23–24M debt** (sources differ) [25], [26]. **US$1.4B GMV** (value of deals) in 2024, **more than 1M monthly visitors**, **3,000+ brokerages** on Nawy Partners [25]. It says it is profitable (company claim).
- **Gap to target:** mostly compounds from about 150 developers [25]. Little coverage of resale flats in normal neighbourhoods and Upper Egypt.

### Dubizzle Egypt (formerly OLX Egypt) — دوبيزل

- **What it is:** a general classifieds site (cars, phones, property). OLX Egypt was renamed Dubizzle [37].
- **Key features:** property categories, "verified accounts first" option, paid "Featured", "Elite" and "Property of the Week" ads, "Post your ad in 3 easy steps" [37]. A mortgage calculator from Banque Misr was added in July 2025 [38].
- **How it earns:** **paid featured ads and packages**. Prices vary by category and city [42], and Egypt prices are **not public**. It also has bank partnerships [38].
- **Scale:** "200,000+" property ads. Apartments for sale: 108,914; villas for sale: 55,649; apartments for rent: 39,601 [37]. Dubizzle says its property section gets **2.8M+ monthly visits** [39] (check).
- **Gap to target:** anyone can post, so quality is mixed. It is a general marketplace, not a home-search guide.

### Bayut Egypt (بيوت)

- **What it is:** a property portal from Dubizzle Group, launched in Egypt in **October 2023** [40].
- **Key features:** TruBroker badge for good agents, new projects, map view, search by drive time, "Find My Agent" [41]. The TruCheck verification badge is documented only for Dubai and Abu Dhabi, not Egypt.
- **How it earns:** owners can list directly. Agencies and developers pay for visibility and leads. Prices are **not public** [41].
- **Scale:** it claims **10 million active users** in its second year (check, company blog, undated) [41].
- **Gap to target:** a newcomer. It overlaps with its sister site Dubizzle.

### Facebook groups and Marketplace (فيسبوك)

- **What it is:** free posts in local groups ("شقق للإيجار في …") and on Marketplace.
- **Features:** free, local, fast, in Arabic, and people can message the poster directly.
- **How it earns:** paid Facebook ads (boosted posts). Facebook ads can reach about **51.6M** people in Egypt [5].
- **Scale:** a government property blog calls Facebook "the most dominant platform for real estate ads in Egypt" [51] (check: promotional source, no data).
- **Gap to target:** no verification, duplicate posts, prices often hidden ("السعر في الخاص"), and hard to search by city or type.

### New: the government platform (realestate.gov.eg and "Property Egypt")

- The Ministry of Housing launched an official MLS-based platform in **February 2025**. It promises to remove **duplicate and fake listings** [24].
- In **December 2025**, "Property Egypt" launched for foreigners and Egyptians abroad. It starts with **3,000+ units in New Alamein**, each with an ID linked to the property registry [23].
- **Meaning for us:** the government agrees that trust is the problem. It also focuses on state and developer projects, not private flats in Aswan.

## 3. Buyer behaviour | سلوك المشتري

**Warning: the evidence here is weak.** There is no good public survey of how Egyptians search for homes. Most of what exists comes from companies selling something. This is the best section to strengthen with your own interviews (see the end).

### How people search today

- **Brokers (السمسار):** still central. Nawy's CEO said the market is "dominated by agents with offline relationships". Buyers had to visit developers one by one, which he called "highly inefficient" [25].
- **Brokers in cities:** a Cairo living guide says brokers usually cover specific areas and stand between renter and landlord. It also describes the trick of showing bad flats first [50] (check, anecdotal).
- **Facebook:** said to be the main channel for property ads [51] (check).
- **Portals:** Aqarmap claims 1M+ users [28], Dubizzle 2.8M monthly property visits [39], Nawy 1M+ monthly visitors [25]. So millions do search online, but we do not know how many finish a deal online.

### Trust problems

- **Duplicate and fake listings:** the Housing Ministry's own platform launch says its MLS "eliminates duplicate and fake listings" [24]. That means the government admits the problem exists. It gives no numbers.
- **Scams:** the Interior Ministry arrested a man who advertised **fake housing units** in Cairo on social media and used forged contracts [52] (check: date not clear).
- **Unlicensed brokers:** Parliament amended the brokers' law in **March 2022** to bring order to the market. An earlier draft had a **5% fee ceiling** [45] (check). Typical commission rates are **unknown**. Do not quote "2.5%" without a source.
- **Price opacity:** many posts hide the price. Portals answer with price guides (Aqarmap [28]). Installment prices can be much higher than cash prices. Naguib Sawiris claimed the cash price is a quarter of what you pay over 10 years [44] (check: one person's opinion).

### Installment plans (التقسيط)

- Developers sell on long plans: **5% down + 10 years** (Mountain View, El Attal), **1.5% down + 12 years** (Madinet Masr, Palm Hills), up to **15–16 years** in some offers [43], [32] (check each developer).
- Why: bank mortgages cost about **24.5%** [9]. Nawy's CEO says banks rarely lend for homes [25].
- **For manyhouses:** show the **installment** (monthly amount and years), not only the total price. People compare monthly payments.

### Egyptians abroad and foreigners

- Record remittances: **US$36.5B** [20]. The government built "Property Egypt" for Egyptians abroad and foreigners [23]. An Asharq Business survey noted strong interest from Egyptians working abroad after the devaluation [55] (check).
- Egyptians are also buying in **Dubai**. Purchases there rose 150% in early 2025, per the agency Better Homes [56] (check).
- **For manyhouses:** an expat cannot visit easily. Clear photos, a map and a contract draft help them.

### Mobile-first

- **96M+** mobile broadband subscriptions [7]. All major portals push their apps [28], [32], [27]. Property Finder new-project cards use a **WhatsApp** button [32].
- **For manyhouses:** the Streamlit app works in a phone browser. Add a WhatsApp contact button when there are real partners.

## 4. Revenue models | نماذج الربح

### How portals earn money

| Model | How it works | Example | Price evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Agent/broker subscription** | Agents pay monthly or yearly to list | Rightmove (UK), Property Finder, Aqarmap | Rightmove: **£1,621 per advertiser per month** (2025) [48]. Property Finder: "most revenue" from B2B subscriptions [35]. Egypt prices unknown |
| **Credits / pay per listing** | Buy credits and spend them per listing | Property Finder "PF Flex" | Price not public (check) |
| **Featured / promoted ads** | Free basic ad, pay to be on top | Dubizzle "Featured", "Elite" [37], [42]. Aqarmap packages [28] | Aqarmap EGP 100–400 per ad in 2016 [30] (check) |
| **Lead or referral fee** | Portal sends a buyer, takes a share of the agent's commission when the deal closes | Zillow Flex (US) | Reported **35–40%** of the agent's fee [49] (check) |
| **Brokerage commission** | Platform is the broker and earns from the developer or seller | Nawy [25] | Rate not public |
| **Financing / partner fees** | Mortgage or installment products, or bank partnerships | Nawy Now [25]. Dubizzle × Banque Misr [38] | Not public |
| **Developer advertising and events** | Developers pay for launches, banners, expos | Aqarmap expo success fee (check), Property Finder open house [33] | Not public |

Egypt-specific prices are **not published** by any portal. Ask their sales teams, or open "post an ad" on Dubizzle and Aqarmap to see today's EGP prices (see the questions at the end).

### Three options for a near-zero-budget student startup

**Option A: free to list, pay to stand out (featured listings)**
- How: owners and brokers list for free. Later, a small fee pins a listing to the top of its city for a week.
- Pros: no sales team needed. It is easy to explain and matches what users already know from Dubizzle [37]. Free listing helps fill the empty site first.
- Cons: it only makes money once there is traffic. Small fees mean small income. Needs online payment (Fawry, InstaPay, cards) or manual collection.

**Option B: "founding partner" broker plan (freemium subscription)**
- How: the first 10–20 brokers per city list for free for 6 months and get a "Founding partner" badge. After that, a low monthly fee.
- Pros: matches the pitch ask ("brokers/owners partner to list the first real properties"). Brokers bring real listings and check them. Recurring money, like Rightmove and Property Finder [48], [35].
- Cons: brokers already pay for bigger portals with more traffic, so we must show leads. Needs someone to visit and sign brokers (the student team).

**Option C: success fee per closed deal (shared commission)**
- How: no fee to list. If a buyer from manyhouses signs a deal, the partner broker shares a small part of their commission. The contract-draft feature can record that the lead came from manyhouses.
- Pros: brokers pay only when they earn, so it is low risk for them. Similar to Zillow Flex [49] and Nawy's broker deals [25].
- Cons: hard to track whether a deal really closed. Payment takes months. It needs written agreements, and broker rules (licensing, fee limits) must be checked [45].

**Recommendation for the pitch:** start with **B (free founding partners) + A (featured listings later)**. Keep **C** for partners who refuse to pay upfront. Do not promise financing. That needs a bank or a licensed finance company.

## Open questions for the student to check locally

Ask **5 people who searched for a flat in the last 12 months** (ideally in Aswan and one other city):

1. How did you find the flat you took: a broker, Facebook, a portal (which one?), or family and friends?
2. How many places did you visit before choosing? How long did the search take?
3. Did you ever see a fake, already-taken, or wrong-price listing? What happened?
4. How much did the broker charge (percentage or months of rent)? Who paid, buyer or owner?
5. Was the price written in the ad, or did you have to ask in private?
6. Would you trust a listing more if a known local broker had "checked" it? What would make you trust it?
7. Did you search on your phone or a computer? Did you contact the owner by phone call or WhatsApp?
8. For buyers: cash or installments? How many years?

Ask **3 local brokers or owners**:

9. Do you pay for Aqarmap, Property Finder, Dubizzle, or Facebook ads? How much per month? (This fills the "price not public" gaps.)
10. Would you list 3–5 properties for free on manyhouses for 6 months in exchange for leads?
11. Are you registered as a broker (GOEIC / Ministry of Trade)? Is there a fee cap you follow?

Check yourself:

12. Open "post an ad" on Dubizzle Egypt and Aqarmap and screenshot today's EGP prices for featured listings.
13. Count property ads for Aswan on Dubizzle, Aqarmap and Property Finder. Compare with Cairo (Dubizzle Cairo ≈ 156,690 [37]).
14. Find the CAPMAS bulletin itself for 2024 marriages (the figures here come from press coverage of it).
